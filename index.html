Solar questions

A few things worth knowing before you start.

Have another question?

Talk to us about your home ↗
What happens when the sun isn't shining?

Solar output changes with daylight and weather. At night, panels are not generating electricity. A battery can store excess solar energy generated earlier for use later, while grid-connected homes can draw electricity from the grid when required.

Do I need a battery?

Not every solar system needs a battery. Whether storage makes sense depends on how and when your household uses electricity, what you want from the system and how the system is designed.

Will solar take my home off-grid?

Not necessarily. Most residential solar systems remain connected to the electricity grid. Your home can use the energy generated by your solar system while retaining the grid connection when additional power is needed.

Does every roof suit the same solar setup?

No. Roof orientation, available area, pitch, shading and surrounding obstructions can all affect the design. That's why we assess the home before deciding on a system.

How do I know what size system I need?

System size should be considered alongside your energy use, the characteristics of your home and what you want the system to achieve. There isn't one system size that suits every household.

What happens to excess solar generation?

A battery can store available excess energy for use later. A grid-connected system may also export excess electricity, depending on the system and your electricity arrangement.
